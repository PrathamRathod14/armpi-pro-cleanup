How the clean-up robot works

An animation of the logic in cleanup.py: search, drive, pick up, place on the left, repeat. This is a simulation, not a live view of your robot, and it is tidier than real life: the real robot is slower and will miss sometimes.

Moved to the left: 0 of 3

The floor, seen from above

The shaded wedge is what the camera can see. The robot has no map: it only reacts to what is inside that wedge. The real camera spots small objects only within about a metre, so the real floor area must be small.

Here, objects marked "moved" are ignored. The real robot cannot tell a moved object from a new one, so it simply stops after three pick-ups.
